++++ STREETGP (STGP): A Bike Concept Many People Look Down On ++++

This is a personal blog about StreetGP, a motorbike concept culture where small underbone
bikes are built to look like a MotoGP-style race bike. This blog explains which bikes are valid, 
and the "kamote" label that always sticks to the community.

----- I am Cris Manuel A. Mataya, the author of this blog.
